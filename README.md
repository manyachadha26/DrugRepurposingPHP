# Repurposing antidiabetic drugs as PDE4D inhibitors for pseudohypoparathyroidism

Course project, UBT521 (AI in Biotechnology), Thapar Institute of Engineering
and Technology, Patiala.

## The question

In pseudohypoparathyroidism, GNAS defects leave Gsa unable to carry the
parathyroid hormone signal inward, so cyclic AMP never rises and the kidney
cannot respond to a hormone that is present in normal amounts. Current
treatment replaces calcium and vitamin D at the end of the chain and does
nothing about the break in the middle.

cAMP is made by adenylyl cyclase and destroyed by phosphodiesterases. If
production cannot be restored, slowing destruction is the other lever. So:
among approved antidiabetic drugs, is there one that binds the
phosphodiesterase that degrades cAMP in the proximal tubule?

## Headline results

1. **PDE4D dominates the human proximal tubule**, 4,114 nCPM against 657 for
   the next highest phosphodiesterase (Human Protein Atlas v25.1).
2. **Docking scores cannot rank PDE4D binders.** AUC 0.604 for Vina and 0.586
   for Vinardo on 146 actives and 2,246 property-matched decoys, where 0.50 is
   random. No potency is predicted anywhere in this work as a result.
3. **Ranking by interaction pattern does work.** AUC 0.822, 95% CI 0.780 to
   0.864, under leave-one-scaffold-out validation, against 0.656 for a control
   using six physicochemical descriptors alone (DeLong p = 1.4e-9).
4. **Deleting the metal-coordinating waters is invisible to both conventional
   checks.** It pulls docked ligands into the vacated coordination shell, yet
   re-docking RMSD changes by 0.014 A and benchmark enrichment is
   statistically indistinguishable (p = 0.44). Demonstrated across four
   metalloenzymes.
5. **One candidate: sitagliptin.** It reproduces the canonical PDE4 binding
   mode in both PDE4D and PDE4B, ranks above two clinically characterised PDE4
   inhibitors, and is actively secreted into the proximal tubule.

## What this work does NOT claim

1. **Sitagliptin is not a predicted PDE4D inhibitor.** No IC50, Ki, Kd or
   binding affinity is predicted, and no ordering of compounds is a potency
   ranking. It is a hypothesis for an enzyme assay.
2. **AUC 0.822 is not the probability that a high-ranking compound inhibits
   PDE4D.** It is discrimination of known actives from property-matched
   decoys, and nothing more.
3. **Nothing here was tested experimentally.** No assay, no cells, no animals.

Three further limits are stated rather than buried: the docking score failed
its pre-registered gate, a ligand-based model cannot corroborate the result
because it is structurally incapable of scoring a scaffold hop, and one known
inhibitor (cilomilast) is mis-ranked by the method.

## Structures used

| PDB | Protein | Gate 1, top-ranked pose | Used |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1XOQ | PDE4D | 0.756 A | yes, main target |
| 1XMU | **PDE4B** | 0.927 A | yes, isoform counter-screen |
| 7CBQ | PDE4D | 3.511 A | no, failed Gate 1 |
| 3HR1 | PDE10A | 0.617 A | yes, generalisation |
| 1UDT | PDE5A | 8.280 A | no, failed Gate 1 |
| 1A42 | Carbonic anhydrase II | 4.388 A | no, failed Gate 1 |

**1XMU is PDE4B, not PDE4D** (UniProt Q07343). The cross-structure analysis is
therefore an isoform comparison, not a test of conformational robustness. No
second PDE4D conformation was tested, because the only other PDE4D structure
prepared, 7CBQ, failed Gate 1.

Gate 1 is the RMSD of the **top-ranked** re-docked pose against the
crystallographic ligand, not the best pose anywhere in the output. The pass
mark of 2.0 A was fixed before any structure was tested.

## Receptor preparation

Chain A only; metals kept; **every water within 3.0 A of a metal kept**; all
other waters, buffer and cryoprotectant removed; alternate locations resolved
to variant A; original residue numbering preserved; native ligand extracted to
its own file. Conversion to PDBQT with Meeko 0.8.0, not AutoDockTools, which
requires Python 2 and does not run on Apple Silicon.

The water-retention step is the one that matters and the one that standard
protocols get wrong. See result 4 above.

## Reproducing

    conda env create -f environment.yml
    conda activate phppde4

`environment.lock.yml` pins the exact versions used. The headline number is a
scikit-learn fit, so the version matters.

Rounds 1 to 3 of the benchmark are superseded by round 4, which is the result
reported. All four rounds are documented; round 4 is the one to reproduce.
